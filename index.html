Today

I’m a mobile engineer based in Shanghai, currently leading end-to-end iOS delivery for a financial product serving the Mexican market.

Background

Nearly nine years of iOS work taught me that good engineering is more than code. It is architecture that stays clear, interactions that feel effortless and a release process the whole team can trust.

Growth

I started with UIKit and Objective-C, grew with Swift, SwiftUI and reactive architectures, then expanded into Flutter and product design. That range helps me move comfortably between user needs, visual detail and implementation constraints.

AI Native

I use AI coding workflows to move from idea to working prototype, generate engineering scaffolds and automate repetitive delivery work. I have built reusable review and release skills covering linting, tests, signing checks, IPA/OTA artifacts and CI/CD checks.

Goal

I want to build useful products with calm interfaces — software that feels thoughtful on day one and remains dependable after years of change.
